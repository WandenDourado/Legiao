# Blue Wizard — Character Bible

## Reference And Silhouette

Young, compact mage with warm light skin, large expressive brown eyes, brown bangs beneath an oversized pointed navy hood, a knee-length robe, visible dark brown boots, and a tall twisted wooden staff. The staff is always held on the same body side established by the supplied reference; in the front (S) view it appears at viewer-right.

## Palette And Costume

- Deep navy-to-blue hood, mantle, and robe with antique-gold ornamental embroidery and trim.
- Brown leather belt with a round brass buckle, two pouches, and small strap details.
- Dark brown boots and staff; warm skin and brown hair.
- A small faceted cyan crystal held in dark claw-like prongs at the staff head.

## Production Contract

- One shared frame size: 128x192 pixels, RGBA PNG.
- Eight walk frames per direction, ordered contact, recoil, passing, high point, then repeat.
- Five rows in renderer order: S, SW, W, N, NW. East-facing directions are mirrored at runtime.
- Feet share a stable baseline. The staff remains complete, attached to the character, and on the established side.

## Forbidden Drift

Do not alter the character's apparent age, compact proportions, hood silhouette, blue-and-gold robe palette, staff side, leather accessories, cyan crystal color, or full-body framing. Do not introduce text, backgrounds, cast shadows, cropped body parts, duplicate limbs, or stray magical effects.
