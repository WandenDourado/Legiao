# Blue Wizard Character Bible

## Silhouette

Young compact mage with a large hood, knee-length robe, clear boots, and a tall wooden staff. The staff stays on the character's right body side throughout the walk cycle.

## Clothing And Palette

- Deep blue hood and robe with gold trim and ornamental embroidery.
- Brown leather belt, pouch, boots, and small strap accents.
- Warm skin, brown hair visible under the hood, large expressive eyes.
- Cyan crystal glow at the staff head as a small readable accent.

## Scale And Frames

- Legiao legacy compatibility: 165x246 px per frame.
- Walk animation: 6 frames per direction.
- Transparent PNG output.
- Foot-center origin with a stable shared floor baseline.

## Forbidden Drift

- Do not change the character age, body proportions, hood shape, robe color, staff side, or crystal color.
- Do not add backgrounds, labels, cast shadows, or cropped staff/robe parts.
- Embroidery may be simplified for sprite readability, but gold trim placement and color rhythm should remain consistent.
